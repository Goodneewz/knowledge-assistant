Mama Tee's Bakery - Opening Hours

We are open Monday to Saturday from 8:00 AM to 7:00 PM. On Sundays we open from 12:00 PM to 6:00 PM.
We are closed on public holidays, with one exception: on Christmas Eve we open from 8:00 AM to 3:00 PM.
Custom cake orders can be collected at any time during opening hours.
