Mama Tee's Bakery - Refunds and Returns:

Fresh baked goods cannot be returned. If your order arrives damaged or wrong, send a photo within 2 hours of delivery.
We will then offer a replacement or a refund. Approved refunds are paid within 3 working days to your original payment method.
Cake deposits are refundable only if you cancel at least 72 hours before pickup. Later cancellations forfeit the deposit.
